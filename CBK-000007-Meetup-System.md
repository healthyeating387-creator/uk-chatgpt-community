# CBK-000007 — Meetup System

**Project:** UK ChatGPT Community  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Stage 0 — Founder  
**Type:** Community Operations and Physical Engagement Specification

## 1. Purpose

The Meetup System defines how the UK ChatGPT Community helps people meet in person, learn together, build projects, and develop lasting local communities.

The website is not only an online destination.

It is infrastructure for real-world relationships.

## 2. Core Principle

> **Digital infrastructure connects people. Physical experiences make the community real.**

## 3. Objectives

The Meetup System should make it possible to:

- discover nearby events;
- attend events;
- organise events;
- create local hubs;
- meet people with shared interests;
- turn conversations into projects;
- document useful outcomes;
- grow local communities safely.

## 4. Meetup Lifecycle

Every official meetup follows:

**Idea → Proposal → Review → Publish → Registration → Event → Follow-up → Documentation → Outcome → Review**

## 5. Finding a Venue

The community does not initially need to own physical premises.

Possible venues include:

- universities;
- libraries;
- makerspaces;
- coworking spaces;
- technology centres;
- cafés with suitable space;
- company offices;
- community centres;
- robotics facilities;
- partner venues.

Venue arrangements must be appropriate for the expected activity and attendance.

## 6. Meetup Proposal

A prospective organiser should provide:

- proposed title;
- city;
- theme;
- proposed date;
- approximate attendance;
- organiser;
- venue;
- venue status;
- description;
- equipment requirements;
- accessibility requirements;
- expected outcome.

## 7. Meetup Review

Before an event becomes an official community event, the organiser or responsible community lead should check:

- appropriate purpose;
- suitable venue;
- sufficient organiser capacity;
- safety considerations;
- accessibility;
- realistic attendance;
- communication plan.

Small low-risk community events may use a lightweight review.

Higher-risk or larger events require stronger review.

## 8. Meetup Categories

Possible categories include:

### AI Build Night

Members bring laptops and work together on practical projects.

### Robotics Night

Robotics demonstrations, experiments and discussions.

### AI + Sport

AI, football, computer vision, sports analytics and robotics.

### AI for Business

Businesses bring practical problems and builders explore possible solutions.

### University × Industry

Students, researchers, engineers and companies meet.

### Research Evening

Researchers present work, questions or open problems.

### Automation Night

Industrial automation, software, robotics and process improvement.

### Demo Day

Community members demonstrate projects they have built.
