# CBK-000006 — Website v0 + Physical Community

**Project:** UK ChatGPT Community  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Stage 0 — Founder  
**Type:** Website Product Specification

## 1. Purpose

The UK ChatGPT Community website is the public digital home of the community.

Its purpose is not simply to publish articles.

It must help people:

- discover the community;
- meet other people;
- find physical events;
- create local meetups;
- discover projects;
- learn;
- contribute;
- find collaborators;
- connect with universities and companies.

The website is the digital infrastructure that helps create a real-world community.

## 2. Core Principle

> **Digital infrastructure connects people. Physical experiences make the community real.**

The website must therefore treat physical community activity as a core function rather than an optional feature.

## 3. Website v0 Philosophy

Website v0 must be:

- professional;
- modern;
- welcoming;
- fast;
- accessible;
- mobile-friendly;
- easy to understand;
- inexpensive to operate;
- easy for future contributors to maintain.

It should look impressive without requiring a complicated technical system.

## 4. Primary User

The first-time visitor should understand within a few minutes:

1. what the community is;
2. why it exists;
3. what people are doing;
4. how to meet people;
5. how to contribute.

## 5. Main Navigation

Initial navigation:

- Home
- Community
- Meet
- Build
- Learn
- Research
- DRAC
- Become a Builder
- GitHub
- About

Future navigation may expand when justified by demand.

## 6. Home

The homepage is the front door.

It should immediately communicate:

**UK ChatGPT Community**

**Stronger, All Together.**

Supporting message:

> An independent UK community for people exploring ChatGPT, AI, robotics, automation, research and related technologies.

Primary actions:

- Find a Meetup
- Join the Community
- Explore Projects
- Become a Builder

## 7. Live Community Area

The homepage should show activity rather than only static information.

Possible sections:

- Upcoming Meetups
- Active Projects
- Latest Community News
- New Opportunities
- Featured Builder
- Featured Project
- Latest Research
- Local Activity

The first version may use manually maintained content.

Automation comes later.

## 8. Meet

Meet is a primary area of the website.

It answers:

> **Where can I meet people interested in AI, robotics, automation and technology?**

Features should eventually include:

- upcoming events;
- city;
- venue;
- date;
- time;
- theme;
- organiser;
- attendance information;
- registration;
- accessibility information;
- event description.

## 9. Meetup Types

Possible formats include:

### AI Build Night

People bring laptops and build projects together.

### Robotics Night

Robots, prototypes, demonstrations and technical discussion.

### AI + Football

AI, computer vision, sports analytics, robotics and technology.

### AI for Business

Businesses present real problems and builders explore solutions.

### University AI Night

Students, researchers and engineers meet.

### Research Evening
