Independent. Open. Collaborative.

UK ChatGPT Community is an independent community project. We are interested in ChatGPT and OpenAI technologies while remaining independent unless an official relationship is established.

Our aim is to bring together citizens, builders, researchers, universities, companies and organisations to explore responsible innovation in AI, robotics and automation.
